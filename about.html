About EduFinance

EduFinance was created to solve student financial management challenges. Students receive lump-sum funds (loans, grants, scholarships) at the start of semesters and must stretch them across months of rent, tuition, books, and living expenses.

Our platform provides smart semester planners, category budget limits, savings goals, and secure cloud synchronization to ensure every student graduates with financial confidence.
