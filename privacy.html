Privacy Policy

We keep the data we collect to a minimum and we never sell it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

EduFinance is a student budgeting and semester planning web app designed and operated by Hilltop Visuals. In this policy, "EduFinance", "we", "us" and "our" refer to that service and its operators.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, contact us at support@edufinance.app.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the app.

CategoryExamplesWhere it is kept
ProfileName, email address, date of birth, chosen currency, sign-in methodFirebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore
Financial entriesTransactions, budgets, savings goals, amounts owed and owing, semester plan detailsCloud Firestore, under your account
Device settingsTheme choice, app-lock settings, sign-in stateYour browser storage on your device
Messages you send usName, email, subject and message from the contact form, and your email if you join the newsletterCloud Firestore

We do not ask for bank logins and we do not connect to your bank accounts. The amounts in the app are the ones you enter.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store and display your financial entries and generate budgets, plans, insights and reports.
  • To respond to messages and support requests.
  • To send newsletter updates if you subscribe, which you can stop at any time.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the service.

We do not use your financial entries for advertising.

4. Firebase Authentication and Firestore

EduFinance uses Google Firebase. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in with email or Google. Cloud Firestore stores your profile and financial entries under your own account.

Firestore security rules are configured so that signed-in users can only read and write their own records. Data is processed by Google as our service provider in line with its own terms and privacy commitments.

5. Google Gemini and the optional AI insights

AI insights are optional and off until you turn them on in the Insights section. When on, EduFinance sends Google Gemini a summary of your figures (category totals, budgets, goals, monthly history and app activity) and your most recent transactions (date, amount, category and a short description) so it can write your insight sentences and answer questions you ask in the chat. Your full transaction history is not sent. Chat messages you type are sent too. You can turn AI insights off at any time and clear the chat from the chat window; the chat history is kept in your browser on your device.

The numbers themselves, including spending pace, month-end forecast, unusual spending, "Can I afford it?" and "What if?", are calculated from your own records within the app.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us run EduFinance, such as Google Firebase and, for the optional AI review, Google Gemini, and where the law requires it.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can use Reset all data in Settings to remove your financial entries. To have your account and remaining data deleted, or to ask about data in the contact form or newsletter list, email support@edufinance.app.

We may keep limited information where the law requires it or where it is needed to resolve disputes and keep the service secure.

8. Security

We use reputable infrastructure, per-user data separation and access rules to protect your information. You can add an optional app lock on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your devices protected.

9. Children

EduFinance is intended for people aged 13 and over. Sign-up asks for a date of birth and does not allow accounts for people under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. International users and currencies

EduFinance is available to students in many countries and supports more than 30 currencies. Your data is processed on servers operated by our providers, which may be located outside your country. By using the service you understand that your data may be transferred and processed in those locations.

11. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies. EduFinance uses your browser's local storage to remember settings such as your theme, your app-lock settings and whether you are signed in. Firebase may also use storage or cookies that are needed for secure sign-in. You can clear this data in your browser settings, which may sign you out and reset these preferences.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can export your transactions from within the app. For any other request, contact support@edufinance.app and we will respond as soon as we can.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@edufinance.app or use the contact form.
