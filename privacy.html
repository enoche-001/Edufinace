Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

EduFinance collects basic profile information (username, name, date of birth, account ID) and financial tracking data (transactions, budgets, savings goals) required to provide budgeting services.

2. Data Security

All cloud-synced data is secured via Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules, ensuring only authorized account holders can access their financial records.

3. Age Restriction

EduFinance requires users to be at least 13 years of age. Accounts belonging to users under 13 are prohibited.
