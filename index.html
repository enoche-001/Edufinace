Features

Everything you need to stay in control

Practical tools for real student budgets, designed to be clear on the first day and useful all semester.

Semester Cashflow Planner

Spread funding across the weeks ahead and see a safe weekly amount to spend.

Category Budgets

Set limits for food, transport and study, with clear progress and early warnings.

Savings Goals

Name a goal, set a target and watch progress grow with each contribution.

Owe / Owed tracker

Keep a record of money you lent and money you borrowed so nothing is forgotten.

Smart Insights

Plain-language analysis of your own records, so you can see where your money is heading before the month ends.

  • Spending pace
  • Month-end forecast
  • Unusual spending
  • Can I afford it?
  • What if?
Can I afford a $60 purchase?Yes, within budget

Reports

Export transactions as CSV, or open a print-ready report and save it as a PDF.

Multi-currency

Use more than 30 currencies, with amounts formatted correctly for each one.

Dark mode

Switch themes for late-night study sessions. Your choice is remembered on your device.

App lock

Protect the app on a shared or borrowed device with an optional PIN.
