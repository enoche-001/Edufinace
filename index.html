Our Aim & Purpose

College and university students face unique financial pressures—bulk funding from student loans or grants, fluctuating semester expenses, and unpredictable living costs. EduFinance is built to empower students with intelligent cashflow forecasting, robust budgeting tools, and secure cloud tracking so you can focus on your education without financial anxiety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EduFinance free to use?

Yes! EduFinance is completely free for students to track budgets and semester finances.

How does cloud sync work?

EduFinance uses Firebase to securely back up your transactions and settings across devices when signed in.

Can I export my financial reports?

Yes, you can filter transactions by date range and export to CSV or print formatted PDF logs with your unique account ID.
